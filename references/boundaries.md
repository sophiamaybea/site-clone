# Boundaries

A clone is a measured study plus a rebuild the user owns. It is not a license to republish a brand.

Refuse login, paywall, CAPTCHA, DRM bypass, phishing, cookie harvesting, and brand impersonation. Do not submit forms. Stay on the seed origin. Record the source URL of every fetched asset.

| Ship | Allowed | Not allowed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| transfer | Tokens, type scale, spacing rhythm, motion grammar, layout idea | Logo, wordmark, photography set, illustration set, trademarked product shots |
| rebuild | Structure and interaction, original copy and assets | Their headlines passed off as the user's |
| owned | Their assets, if they named ownership or permission | A login wall you crossed to get them |
| inspect | DNA, screenshots, TECH.md | A public replica |

Fonts: record family, weight, and source. Ship a licensed or open face that matches metrics. Do not embed a commercial font file into a transfer.

The published ai-site-cloner note is the same line: owned sites, permission, or inspiration and rebrand. Not a republish.
